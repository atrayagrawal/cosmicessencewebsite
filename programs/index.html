Future Founders Bootcamp

Students

Learn to build. Learn to sell. Eight weeks from a real problem to a product in front of humans — inspired by Naval Ravikant.

Who it's for: Ages 13+ | Duration: 8 Weeks | Format: Live cohort

The Founder & CEO's Walk

Both

298 years of entrepreneurship lessons walked through the streets of Jaipur. History as case study, architecture as metaphor, conversation as classroom.

Who it's for: Students + Adults | Duration: 2 Hours | Format: In-person (Jaipur)

  • Real historical decisions that teach modern strategy
  • Connect Jaipur's past to Apple, Amazon, Tesla, Meta
  • Community and private walk options
